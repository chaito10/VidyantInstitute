# Vidyarthi Platform Orientation — Syllabus

Course ID: `vidyarthi:platform:101` v1.0.0
Credit (descriptive only, §3.3): 1 (vidyarthi-proposed) — does not auto-confer entitlement.
Source spec: `white_paper.md` (from `Vidyarthi_White_Paper.docx` v0.1).

## Outcomes
1. Describe Vidyarthi's local-first architecture and six logical layers.
2. Author a valid versioned course package.
3. Configure a BYOK AI provider without leaking credentials.
4. Explain the Pipelight assessment workflow and evidence model.
5. Distinguish XP, competency, credits, and portable credentials.

## Modules
1. `lessons/01-foundations` — vision, principles, six layers (§1–§2)
2. `lessons/02-content` — courses as versioned Fossil artifacts (§3)
3. `lessons/03-ai-assessment` — BYOK AI and Pipelight assessment (§4–§5)
4. `lessons/04-trust-deploy` — P2P sync, gamification, credentials, deployment (§6–§9)

## Assessment
- Per-lesson `quiz.json` (knowledge checks).

  portable assessment job (`schemas/assessment-job.schema.json`).
- `assessments/project.md` — capstone: publish your own micro-course package.
