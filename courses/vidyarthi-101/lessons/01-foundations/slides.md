## Vidyarthi 101 — Foundations (§1–§2)

Peer-to-peer, AI-driven micro-learning university. Local-first.

---

## Local-first ≠ always decentralized

- Study offline; sync with peers when available
- Minimal node: browser + Fossil repo + local assessment + PocketBase
- Add peer networking and credential replication later

---

## Six layers, one workflow

1. HTML5 client → 2. Fossil courses → 3. PocketBase coordination
2. BYOK AI assistance → 5. Pipelight assessment → 6. Signed credentials

Layers are logical — not mandatory servers.

---

## Principles to remember

Repository-native · human-accountable AI · provider-independent ·
evidence-based · portable · security by design.
